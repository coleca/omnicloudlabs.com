Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

100% Money-Back Guarantee on Digital Research Reports: If a Matched Fund Report does not serve your organization for any reason, email cole@omnicloudlabs.com and we will issue a full refund within 48 hours — no argument and no hoops.

1. Digital Research Reports (e.g., $99 USD Matched Fund Reports)

  • Delivery: Reports are delivered electronically (PDF via email) within 48 hours of payment confirmation.
  • Refund Window & Process: You may request a full 100% refund within 30 days of purchase by emailing cole@omnicloudlabs.com with your organization name or Stripe receipt email.
  • Processing Time: We initiate approved refunds via Stripe within 48 hours of your request. Depending on your card issuer, funds typically appear back on your original payment method within 5–10 business days.
  • Cancellations Before Delivery: If you cancel before your report is delivered, we will immediately cancel the order and refund 100% of your payment.

2. Consulting & Engineering Engagements

For custom cloud platform and AI engineering engagements, cancellation and billing terms are governed by the signed Statement of Work (SOW). Either party may terminate per the notice period in the SOW, with billing limited to work completed through the effective cancellation date.

3. Customer Support & Billing Questions

If you have any billing question, need a receipt, or want to request a refund, please contact us directly before opening a bank dispute so we can resolve it immediately:

  • Email: cole@omnicloudlabs.com or hello@omnicloudlabs.com
  • Response Time: Within 1 business day (24 hours)
  • Business Location: OmniCloud Labs, Florida, United States
