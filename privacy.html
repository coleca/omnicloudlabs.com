Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

OmniCloud Labs ("we," "us," or "our"), based in Florida, United States, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit omnicloudlabs.com, contact us, or purchase a service or digital research report from us.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide directly: Name, organization name, EIN (for nonprofit research reports), email address, and project or support messages you send us.
  • Payment and billing information: Payments are processed securely by Stripe, Inc. We receive your name, email address, billing address (if collected), order details, and payment status from Stripe so we can fulfill your order and process refunds. We never see, collect, or store full credit card numbers on our servers. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Usage and analytics data: Pages visited, referral source, browser/device type, approximate location, and on-page interactions collected via Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity.

2. Analytics & Tracking Technologies

We use two third-party analytics tools to understand site traffic and improve usability:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Collects aggregated traffic and event data using cookies and similar technologies. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Captures anonymized interaction metrics (such as scroll depth, clicks, and session heatmaps) to help us improve site layout and reliability. Sensitive form fields are masked by default. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

3. How We Use Information

  • Deliver consulting engagements and custom digital research reports.
  • Respond to customer support inquiries and process refunds under our guarantee.
  • Maintain accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention records.
  • Measure and improve website performance and content.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain customer order, delivery, and support records only as long as needed to fulfill services, honor refunds, and meet legal or tax obligations. All website traffic is served over HTTPS, and all payment card handling is delegated to PCI-DSS compliant Stripe infrastructure.

5. Your Rights & Opt-Out

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or opt out of any follow-up emails at any time by emailing cole@omnicloudlabs.com or hello@omnicloudlabs.com. We honor all opt-out requests promptly.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact OmniCloud Labs (Florida, United States) at cole@omnicloudlabs.com or hello@omnicloudlabs.com.
